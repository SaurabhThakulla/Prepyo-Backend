Status: 3 original, unofficial tasks, one per Speaking part. This pilot is not
completion of the requested 1,800 new tasks (50 per 36 Practice categories).
No official exam questions, recalled questions or third-party passages were copied.

Official reference: IELTS partners, **IELTS Academic: Speaking test format**,
https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking
(accessed 18 September 2026).

This reference supports task structure and assessment criteria, not authorship
or endorsement of these prompts. There are no external factual claims or data
in the three prompts requiring separate factual citations.

Part 1 covers familiar experience; Part 2 provides a cue card, one minute of
preparation and a one-to-two-minute long turn; Part 3 extends the Part 2 theme
into a general discussion. The 60/120-second Part 1/3 timers are local practice
budgets, not official per-question limits. The pilot is not a complete examiner-led
Speaking test. Speaking has no exact answer key; explanations give response
guidance, not model opinions that learners must reproduce. No stimulus media
is required for these three tasks.

Assessment criteria referenced: fluency and coherence, lexical resource,
grammatical range and accuracy, pronunciation. Automated database checks do
not certify difficulty, linguistic quality, or IELTS scoring equivalence.
Specialist review and live recording/evaluation checks remain necessary.

The migration preserves edits on replay. Its down migration deliberately
preserves questions and learner references. Applying it to a disposable local
database is not a production deployment.
